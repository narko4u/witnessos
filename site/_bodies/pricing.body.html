Before you buy

What a fee does not buy

  • No compliance certificate. We produce evidence and grades. We do not certify you, and a grade is not a legal opinion.
  • No legal advice. Mapping your obligations is your counsel's call, not ours, and we say so in writing.
  • No outcome guarantee. A record cannot prove an outcome happened. E3 records a claim about a response, and we tell you which grade you actually hold.
  • No hidden access. Deployment is self-hosted. Your keys stay in your infrastructure and nothing calls home.

If a proposal does not name a number, ask for one. That is a rule we hold ourselves to, and it applies in reverse: if a number here does not fit your scope, tell us and we will price the scope you actually have.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does WitnessOS do?
WitnessOS is a runtime governance layer for autonomous agents. Every agent action is evaluated against policy before execution and produces a cryptographic receipt with an evidence grade from E0 to E4. E4 requires legitimate E3 corroborated evidence plus independently authenticated external anchoring: an RFC 3161 timestamp that authenticates against operator-provisioned trust roots and remains verifiable offline.
Is there a free way to evaluate it?
Yes. The interactive simulation at demo.html runs entirely in your browser with sample data, no sign-up and no install. The verifier, the MCP validator and the passport are free and open source, and you can run them against your own artifacts today.
Why are some prices a range?
Because scope moves the number and we would rather show you the floor than hide it. The starting figure covers a defined scope; anything beyond it is quoted in writing before work starts. Nothing begins on an open-ended estimate.
Do the open-source tools feed data back to you?
No. The verifier runs with no gateway, no credentials and no network. The MCP validator is standard library only. Nothing phones home, and there is no telemetry to disable.
How do I get access to the gateway engine?
The gateway engine is proprietary and released through our design-partner programme under NDA. Request access and we will schedule a short intro call to understand your deployment and use case.
Can I self-host WitnessOS?
Yes. The gateway is designed to run on your own infrastructure, fully self-hosted with no data egress, and is deployed that way with design partners today. Encryption keys are generated and stored on your infrastructure.
What connectors are available now?
Gmail dispatch for human-in-the-loop approvals and Stripe payment processing are live today. Additional email, messaging and financial-system connectors are on the roadmap and ship through the design-partner programme.
What is your patent status?
Patent pending covers our runtime guardrail methodology for autonomous AI agents. The patent protects our implementation, not your data; your audit trails, policies and agent configurations remain your intellectual property.
