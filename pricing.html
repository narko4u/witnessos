Frequently asked questions

What counts as an action?
An action is any tool call your agent makes - an API request, a shell command, a file operation, a database query, etc. Each action is evaluated against your policies and logged in the audit trail. Actions are counted per-tenant across all agents.
Can I change plans mid-month?
Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated for the remainder of the billing cycle. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle.
Do you offer annual pricing?
Yes. Annual plans save up to 20% compared to monthly billing. See the per-year pricing on each plan card above.
Is there a free trial?
The Free tier is always available with no credit card required. Starter and Pro plans come with a 14-day free trial. No commitment, cancel anytime.
What happens if I exceed my action limit?
Agent actions are paused until the next billing cycle or until you upgrade your plan. Your audit trail and policies remain intact. We notify you at 80%, 90%, and 100% of your limit.
Can I self-host WitnessOS?
Self-hosted deployment is available on Starter and Pro plans. You deploy the WitnessOS gateway on your infrastructure and manage your own encryption keys. We provide Docker images, deployment scripts, and documentation.
What is your patent status?
Patent pending covers our runtime guardrail methodology for autonomous AI agents. You're covered under our license to use WitnessOS as published. The patent protects our IP, not your data - your audit trails, policies, and agent configurations remain your intellectual property.
