### 1. Purpose

The Parties wish to evaluate a potential business, technical, or investment relationship concerning autonomous agent governance technology (the "**Purpose**"). This Agreement governs the disclosure and protection of Confidential Information exchanged in connection with that evaluation.

### 2. Definition of Confidential Information

**"Confidential Information"** means any non-public information disclosed by one Party (Discloser) to the other Party (Recipient), whether orally, in writing, or in any other form, that:

(a) is marked or identified as confidential or proprietary at the time of disclosure; or

(b) by the nature of the information, the circumstances of disclosure, or the relationship between the Parties, would reasonably be understood to be confidential.

**Without limiting the foregoing**, Confidential Information of Empire Labs Pty Ltd includes but is not limited to:

(i) Source code, object code, algorithms, software architecture, and system designs contained in Empire Labs' proprietary gateway engine repository and any related private repositories;

(ii) Internal security assessments, threat models, and penetration test results not already published;

(iii) Business plans, pricing models, customer lists, partnership terms, and revenue data;

(iv) Patent filings, provisional specifications, and intellectual property strategies;

(v) Any information derived from, extracted from, or summarising the above.

### 3. Exclusions

Confidential Information does **not** include information that:

(a) is or becomes publicly known through no breach of this Agreement by Recipient;

(b) was in Recipient's lawful possession before disclosure, as evidenced by written records;

(c) is independently developed by Recipient without use of or reference to Confidential Information, as evidenced by written records;

(d) is rightfully obtained by Recipient from a third party without restriction.

### 4. Obligations of Recipient

Recipient agrees to:

(a) **Hold in confidence** all Confidential Information and exercise at least the same degree of care used to protect its own confidential information (but no less than reasonable care);

(b) **Use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose** and for no other commercial or competitive purpose;

(c) **Limit access** to individuals within its organisation who have a genuine need to know for the Purpose and who are bound by equivalent confidentiality obligations;

(d) **Not copy, reverse engineer, decompile, or disassemble** any software, code, or technical materials provided as Confidential Information, except to the extent reasonably necessary to evaluate the Purpose;

(e) **Not disclose Confidential Information to any third party** without the Discloser's prior written consent;

(f) **Notify Discloser promptly** upon becoming aware of any unauthorised access, use, or disclosure.

### 5. Permitted Disclosures

Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law, regulation, or court order, provided that:

(a) Recipient gives Discloser prompt written notice to allow Discloser to seek a protective order;

(b) Recipient discloses only the minimum information required; and

(c) Recipient cooperates with Discloser's reasonable efforts to obtain confidential treatment.

### 6. Term

(a) This Agreement commences on the date above and continues for **two (2) years** (the "**Term**").

(b) Confidentiality obligations for **trade secrets** and **source code** survive indefinitely.

(c) Confidentiality obligations for all other Confidential Information survive for **five (5) years** from the date of disclosure.

### 7. Return or Destruction

Upon Discloser's written request, Recipient shall within **fourteen (14) days**:

(a) Return all copies of tangible Confidential Information; or

(b) Destroy all copies and certify in writing that destruction is complete.

Recipient may retain one archival copy for legal compliance purposes, which remains subject to this Agreement.

### 8. No License or Warranty

(a) No intellectual property rights are granted or implied by this Agreement. All Confidential Information remains the property of Discloser.

(b) All Confidential Information is provided "**as is**" without warranty of any kind. Discloser shall not be liable for any use of Confidential Information by Recipient.

### 9. No Obligation to Proceed

Nothing in this Agreement obligates either Party to proceed with any business relationship, partnership, or transaction. Either Party may discontinue discussions at any time without liability.

### 10. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This Agreement is governed by the laws of **Queensland, Australia**. Each Party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Queensland and the Federal Court of Australia.

### 11. Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties concerning the protection of Confidential Information and supersedes all prior communications, representations, or agreements, whether written or oral.

### 12. Amendments

Any amendment to this Agreement must be in writing and signed by both Parties.
