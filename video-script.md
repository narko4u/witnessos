## Production Notes

**Visual style:** Dark mode (#0d1117 background). Neon purple (#6e62ff) as the primary glow/line colour. Clean, geometric motion graphics with subtle particle effects for the Merkle chain.

**Audio:** Cinematic ambient track - starts minimal, builds through the middle, resolves to an uplifting major key at 1:04. No voice effects - clean, confident VO.

**Tone:** Not fear-based. Not hype-based. Confident, grounded, visionary. The feeling should be: "Finally - someone solved this."

**Runtime:** 90 seconds is tight. Each 6-second chunk has one clear idea. No wasted frames.

**Distribution:** Website hero section, YouTube, LinkedIn, sales prospecting emails, conference booth loop.
