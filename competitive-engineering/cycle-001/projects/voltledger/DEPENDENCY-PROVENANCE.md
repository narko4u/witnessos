## Standards and external references

- AS/NZS 3760 is referenced by name only. Its text and interval tables are not copied, embedded or redistributed. Interval values in the product are supplied by the operator, who holds whatever access to the standard their business requires. This mirrors what the incumbents do and keeps the product a record keeper, not a rule publisher.
- Competitor information used in this cycle: published prices, published feature descriptions and published deployment statements, read from vendor pages on 2026-11-03 and cited inline in dossiers. No competitor code, screenshot, asset, logo, documentation PDF or trial data is reproduced. No competitor trademark is used as an Empire asset.

## What was deliberately not taken

- No competitor source code, decompiled bundles or leaked material.
- No competitor UI assets, icons, copy or screenshots.
- No proprietary model weights. The first release uses no model at all.
- No customer data of any kind. Every record in the harness is synthetic.
- No scraped corpus and no licensed dataset. Nothing to attribute, nothing to revoke.
